DMIT Test

What is Dermatoglyphics Multiple Intelligence Testing?

DMIT is a scientific fingerprint analysis method that helps identify a person's innate potential, learning style, and multiple intelligences — giving students and parents data-backed clarity for academic and career decisions.

1

Fingerprint Scan

A quick, painless scan of all ten fingerprints using certified DMIT scanning equipment.

2

Data Analysis

Fingerprint patterns are analysed using validated DMIT software to map brain lobe development.

3

Detailed Report

A comprehensive report covering learning style, multiple intelligences, and personality traits.

4

Counseling Session

A one-on-one session with me to walk through the report and practical next steps.

Why Parents Choose DMIT

Identify your child's dominant learning style — visual, auditory, or kinesthetic.

Understand innate multiple intelligences to guide subject and career choices.

Reduce academic pressure by aligning expectations with natural strengths.

One-time test — results remain valid for a lifetime as fingerprints don't change.
